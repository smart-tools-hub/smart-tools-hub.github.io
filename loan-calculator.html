What is Loan Calculator?

This Loan Calculator is a free online tool that helps you calculate Equated Monthly Installment EMI for any type of loan. Whether you are planning for personal loan, home loan, car loan or business loan, this calculator shows exact monthly payment amount. You just need to enter three things: loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure in years. The calculator uses standard EMI formula used by banks worldwide to give accurate results. No registration required and works instantly on mobile phones and desktop computers.

How to Use It:

Step 1: Enter Loan Amount in dollars
Step 2: Enter Annual Interest Rate offered by bank
Step 3: Enter Loan Tenure in Years
Step 4: Click "Calculate EMI Now" button

EMI Formula Used by Banks

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n - 1]
Where P = Loan Amount, r = Monthly Interest Rate, n = Total Number of Months
Our calculator automatically converts annual rate to monthly and years to months for accurate results.

FAQs

Q1: What is EMI in loan calculator?
A: EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. Fixed amount you pay to bank every month. EMI includes both principal amount and interest portion.

Q2: Can I use this calculator for mortgage loan?
A: Yes, this loan calculator works for mortgage or home loans also. For USA specific mortgage, use Mortgage Calculator USA.

Q3: Does interest rate change EMI amount?
A: Yes, interest rate has biggest impact on EMI. Even 0.5% difference in interest rate can change monthly payment by hundreds of dollars for large loans.

Q4: Is my loan data saved anywhere?
A: No. Everything runs in your browser. We never store your loan amount or details.
