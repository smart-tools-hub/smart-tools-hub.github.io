Important Percentage Formulas You Should Know

Formula 1: Finding X% of Y = (X / 100) * Y
Example: What is 15% of 800? = (15/100) * 800 = 120

Formula 2: Percentage Increase or Decrease = ((New - Old) / Old) * 100
Example: Price went from $40 to $50. Increase = ((50-40)/40)*100 = 25%

Formula 3: X is What Percent of Y = (X / Y) * 100
Example: 25 is what percent of 100? = (25/100)*100 = 25%
