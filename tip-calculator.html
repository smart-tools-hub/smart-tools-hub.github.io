What is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator is an online tool that helps you calculate how much tip to leave at restaurants, cafes, bars, and for delivery services. Instead of doing math on your phone, just enter your bill amount and get instant tip amounts for 15%, 18%, and 20%.

How to Use This Tip Calculator

Using our free tip calculator is very simple. Follow these 3 steps:

  2. Select Tip Percentage: Choose 15% for good service, 18% for great service, or 20% for excellent service. You can also enter a custom tip percentage.
  3. Split the Bill: If you are dining with friends, enter the number of people to split the tip and total amount equally.

How Much Tip Should You Leave in USA?

In the United States, tipping culture is very common. Here is the standard guide:

  • 15%: Standard for average service at restaurants
  • 18%: For good service
  • 20%: For excellent service
  • 25%+: For outstanding service

For delivery drivers, taxi drivers, and barbers, 15-20% tip is also recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% tip on $100?

20% tip on $100 bill is $20. Total amount with tip will be $120.

What is 20% tip on $20?

20% tip on $20 bill is $4. Total amount with tip will be $24.

How to calculate 20 percent tip?

To calculate 20% tip, multiply your bill amount by 0.20. For example: $50 x 0.20 = $10 tip.

Is 20 percent a good tip?

Yes, 20% is considered an excellent tip in the USA for great restaurant service.
